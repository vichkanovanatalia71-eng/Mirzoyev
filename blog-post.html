How to Maintain Your Dental Implants

M
Dr. Mirzoyev
January 15, 2024 5 min read

Dental implants are a significant investment in your oral health. With proper care, they can last a lifetime. Here's everything you need to know about maintaining your dental implants.

Daily Care Routine

Just like natural teeth, dental implants require regular cleaning. Brush twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush and use a non-abrasive toothpaste. Pay special attention to the area where the implant meets the gum line.

Brushing Technique

Use gentle circular motions around the implant. Avoid aggressive brushing, which can irritate the gum tissue around the implant. An electric toothbrush with a sensitive mode can be particularly effective.

Flossing

Daily flossing is essential. Use unwaxed floss or implant-specific floss to clean around the implant. Water flossers are an excellent supplement to traditional flossing.

Regular Check-ups

Visit your dentist every 6 months for professional cleaning and examination. During these visits, your dentist will check the implant's stability, surrounding bone health, and the condition of the crown.

What to Avoid

  • Hard foods that can damage the crown (ice, hard candy)
  • Smoking, which increases the risk of implant failure
  • Using teeth as tools (opening packages, etc.)
  • Grinding teeth — consider a night guard if applicable

Signs to Watch For

Contact your dentist immediately if you notice any of these symptoms:

  • Pain or discomfort around the implant
  • Swelling or redness of the gums
  • Loosening of the implant or crown
  • Difficulty chewing
