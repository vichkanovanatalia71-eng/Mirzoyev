Everything You Need to Know About Dental Implants

Dr. Mirzoyev | January 15, 2024 | 5 min read
Featured Image

Dental implants have revolutionized modern dentistry, offering a permanent solution for missing teeth that looks, feels, and functions just like natural teeth. Whether you've lost a tooth due to decay, injury, or periodontal disease, dental implants provide a reliable and long-lasting restoration option.

What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone beneath the gum line. This post serves as an artificial tooth root, providing a strong foundation for a replacement tooth (crown). The implant fuses with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration, creating a stable and permanent anchor.

Types of Dental Implants

There are several types of dental implants available, each suited for different situations. Single implants replace individual missing teeth, while implant-supported bridges can replace multiple adjacent teeth. For patients who have lost all their teeth, All-on-4 or All-on-6 solutions provide a full arch of teeth supported by just 4-6 implants.

The Implant Procedure

The implant process typically involves several stages spread over 3-6 months. It begins with a comprehensive consultation and 3D imaging, followed by the surgical placement of the implant. After a healing period of 3-6 months, during which the implant integrates with the bone, a custom crown is attached to complete the restoration.

Recovery and Aftercare

Most patients experience minimal discomfort after implant surgery, manageable with over-the-counter pain medication. Soft foods are recommended for the first few days. Once healed, dental implants require the same care as natural teeth: regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups. With proper care, implants can last a lifetime.

Considering Dental Implants?

If you're interested in dental implants, the first step is a comprehensive consultation. We'll evaluate your oral health, discuss your options, and create a personalized treatment plan.
