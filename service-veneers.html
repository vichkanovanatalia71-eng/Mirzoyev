Porcelain Veneers

Ultra-thin porcelain shells custom-crafted to transform the shape, color, and alignment of your teeth for a flawless smile.

What are Porcelain Veneers?

Porcelain veneers are wafer-thin, custom-made shells of tooth-colored porcelain that are bonded to the front surface of your teeth. They are an ideal solution for correcting chips, cracks, gaps, discoloration, and misalignment, providing you with a natural-looking, radiant smile that can last for decades.

The Procedure

1

Consultation

Comprehensive smile assessment, discussion of your goals, and digital smile design to preview your new look.

2

Preparation

Minimal enamel removal (about 0.5mm) from the tooth surface to create space for the veneer.

3

Impressions

Precise digital or physical impressions taken and sent to our laboratory for custom veneer fabrication.

4

Fitting

Trial placement to check fit, color match, and shape. Adjustments made for perfect alignment and aesthetics.

5

Bonding

Permanent bonding of the porcelain veneers using special dental cement and light curing for a durable, lasting result.

Benefits

  • Natural, translucent appearance matching real teeth
  • Stain-resistant porcelain surface
  • Minimally invasive preparation process
  • Durable — can last 15-20 years with proper care
  • Corrects multiple cosmetic issues at once
  • Custom-designed for your unique facial features
