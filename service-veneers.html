What are Porcelain Veneers?

Porcelain veneers are wafer-thin, custom-made shells of tooth-colored porcelain designed to cover the front surface of teeth. They are bonded to the teeth to change their color, shape, size, or length, providing an immediate and dramatic improvement to your smile. Modern veneers require minimal tooth preparation and can last 15-20 years with proper care.

The Procedure

1

Digital Smile Design

Using advanced digital imaging, we create a preview of your new smile so you can see the expected results before treatment begins.

2

Tooth Preparation

A minimal amount of enamel (0.3-0.5mm) is removed to accommodate the veneer. Impressions are taken for the dental lab.

3

Temporary Veneers

Custom temporary veneers are placed while your permanent veneers are crafted in our partner dental laboratory.

4

Bonding

Your custom veneers are carefully bonded to your teeth using specialized dental cement. Color and fit are verified for perfection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Modern veneer techniques require minimal tooth preparation. Only a thin layer of enamel (0.3-0.5mm) is removed, which preserves the integrity of your natural tooth structure.

With proper care, porcelain veneers typically last 15-20 years. Avoid biting hard objects and maintain good oral hygiene to maximize their lifespan.

Veneers can improve the appearance of mildly crooked or misaligned teeth without the need for braces. For more significant alignment issues, orthodontic treatment may be recommended first.
