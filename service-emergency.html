When Do You Need Emergency Dental Care?

Dental emergencies can happen at any time. Whether it's a severe toothache, a knocked-out tooth, a broken crown, or swelling, our emergency dental team is ready to provide immediate relief and treatment. Don't wait — prompt treatment can save your tooth and prevent complications.

Common Emergency Situations

1

Severe Toothache

Intense, persistent tooth pain that may indicate infection or abscess. We provide rapid diagnosis and pain relief.

2

Knocked-Out Tooth

Time is critical — a knocked-out tooth can often be re-implanted if treated within 30-60 minutes. Keep the tooth moist and come in immediately.

3

Broken or Cracked Tooth

Fractures from trauma or biting hard objects. We assess the damage and provide restoration options from bonding to crowns.

4

Lost Filling or Crown

Exposed tooth structure needs prompt attention to prevent further damage or sensitivity. We can replace or repair restorations same day.
