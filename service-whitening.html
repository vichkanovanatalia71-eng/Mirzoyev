What is Professional Teeth Whitening?

Professional teeth whitening is a safe, effective cosmetic procedure that uses clinical-grade bleaching agents to remove stains and discoloration from your teeth. Unlike over-the-counter products, in-office whitening delivers dramatic results in a single session, with careful monitoring to protect your gums and enamel.

The Procedure

1

Consultation & Shade Assessment

Initial assessment of your teeth shade, oral health check, and discussion of expected results and any sensitivity concerns.

2

Professional Cleaning

Thorough professional cleaning to remove plaque and surface stains, ensuring the whitening gel works evenly across all teeth.

3

Whitening Gel Application

Clinical-grade whitening gel is carefully applied to your teeth while your gums are protected with a barrier shield.

4

Light Activation

A special LED light activates the whitening gel, accelerating the bleaching process for faster and more effective results.

5

Results & Aftercare

Final shade comparison to document your results. Post-treatment care instructions and tips for maintaining your bright smile.
