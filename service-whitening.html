Teeth Whitening

Professional teeth whitening for a brighter, more confident smile. Achieve results up to 8 shades whiter in just one visit.

What is Professional Teeth Whitening?

Professional teeth whitening is a safe, effective cosmetic procedure that uses advanced bleaching agents to remove deep stains and discoloration from your teeth. Unlike over-the-counter products, our in-office treatment delivers dramatic results in a single session under the careful supervision of our dental professionals.

The Procedure

1

Consultation

Initial assessment of your teeth color, oral health check, and discussion of your desired shade and expectations.

2

Cleaning

Thorough professional cleaning to remove plaque and surface stains, ensuring even whitening results.

3

Whitening Application

Professional-grade whitening gel is carefully applied to your teeth with gum protection in place.

4

UV Activation

Special UV light activates the whitening gel, accelerating the bleaching process for optimal results.

5

Results Check

Final shade comparison, aftercare instructions, and tips for maintaining your bright new smile.

Benefits

  • Safe and professionally supervised procedure
